# 02 Reference evidence

No supplied page or protected baseline. Evidence is organized as three visual disciplines, not copied brands or layouts.

## Reference A — watershed documentary cinematography

### Observation

- Wide landscape frames often keep the horizon high or low, leaving one large quiet field for atmosphere (high confidence).
- Water, fog, grasses, and reflected light each move at different speeds; their temporal layering makes a scene feel alive (high confidence).
- Human infrastructure appears as a small trace inside the landscape rather than the dominant object (medium confidence).

### Inference

- A left/top copy-safe zone can coexist with a living focal area if the water line travels diagonally toward the lower right.
- Slow subject motion will support reading better than fast montage.

### Transferable rules

1. Put at least two ecological motion layers in every accepted video; camera motion alone does not count.
2. Preserve one 35–45% quiet zone in Hero frames and keep the ecological focal subject unobstructed.

## Reference B — field notebook and botanical specimen sheets

### Observation

- Handwritten annotations create intimacy when surrounded by large unmarked paper or image space (high confidence).
- Thin rules, small coordinates, dates, and material labels build a sense of observation without card containers (high confidence).
- Ink, off-white paper, moss stains, and graphite form a restrained material family (high confidence).

### Inference

- A small amount of calligraphic Chinese can feel like a field note rather than a ceremonial slogan.
- Section metadata can behave as marginalia and tie otherwise different videos into one system.

### Transferable rules

1. Use expressive Chinese as authored marks across sections, then recover with calm reading text.
2. Replace repeated cards with rules, marginal labels, coordinates, and asymmetric annotations.

## Reference C — ecological succession / seasonal rhythm

### Observation

- Ecological change alternates density and recovery: open water, dense reeds, flowing channels, then cultivated urban edges (medium confidence).
- Greens are rarely uniform; shadow, silt, stone, bark, mist and pale sky occupy more area than saturated foliage (high confidence).
- Seasonal light creates brief high-chroma moments rather than a continuous pure-green field (high confidence).

### Inference

- The page can shift from dark wet morning to bone daylight and back to a quiet dusk ending.
- Section heights should vary with ecological tempo instead of repeating equal modules.

### Transferable rules

1. Keep media-derived neutrals at 75–85% of the long-page silhouette; use vivid chlorophyll green below 12%.
2. Build a section rhythm of immersion → pale recovery → immersion → practical calm ending.

## Selected evidence synthesis

- Dominant media: full-bleed, slow, layered environmental motion.
- Layout: asymmetric field notes on a stable 12-column grid; no equal card wall.
- Type: one strong handwritten/calligraphic Chinese peak plus distributed non-Hero expressive surfaces.
- Color: peat black, fog bone, silt, reed, shallow-water blue; vivid green only as a living signal.
- Responsive: mobile crops preserve water paths and plant motion, while copy moves into measured top/bottom bands without changing media mode.

PASS。三个视觉纪律均提供至少两条可迁移规则，观察、推断与最终决定已分离。
